Augmented reality

See the display in the store before you produce it.

Augmented reality in the browser, with no app: the display appears at full scale on the store floor, on the shelf or on the wall, on the phone of whoever needs to approve it.

75 LAB turns every point of sale (POS) project into an augmented reality link. Anyone who opens it on a phone sees the display, the island or the booth at full scale, right where it will stand. That shortens approval, avoids units that do not fit and helps sell the project to the retail buyer.

How it works

  • iPhone: the link opens straight into the phone's native augmented reality.
  • Android: the link opens in Google's AR viewer, also with no app.
  • Desktop: the model appears in 3D so you can rotate it, with a QR code to continue on your phone.
  • The unit appears at full scale, standing on the floor or mounted on the wall, depending on the type.

What it does for trade marketing, day to day

  • Approve without a PDF: marketing, trade and sales see the same unit to scale.
  • Sell in to retail: the buyer sees the display in their own store before agreeing to the space.
  • Check dimensions: height, depth and aisle, in the real location.
  • Train installers: the assembly page shows the unit in AR next to the step by step instructions.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install an app to see the display in augmented reality?

No. The link opens in the browser on iPhone and Android and uses each system's native AR. On a computer, the 3D model appears with a QR code to open it on your phone.

Does the display appear at full scale?

Yes. The unit opens at real scale, standing on the floor or mounted on the wall. With large booths you can pinch to shrink it and then go back to full scale.

Do I need a 3D model?

No. If the brand already has the 3D file, we prepare it for AR. If not, the model is built as part of the 75 LAB project.

On newer iPhones, part of the display disappears behind objects. Is that a defect?

No. iPhones with a LiDAR sensor hide the virtual unit behind real objects, such as a person or a box, and this cannot be turned off. Just clear the space in front of the camera.
