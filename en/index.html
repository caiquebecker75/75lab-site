Proven point-of-sale agency

A good idea is one that happens.

75 LAB creates, engineers and produces displays, islands, in-store kits and booths, with proof at both ends: you see the display on your shelf in augmented reality before production, and you know where it is installed after it leaves the factory.

75 LAB is a point of sale (POS) and trade marketing agency based at CENU in São Paulo, founded in 2024 by Caique Becker, who came from retail, and Daniel Coimbra, who came from industry. It creates, engineers and produces POS material for consumer goods manufacturers: floor and counter displays, clip strips, promotional islands, end caps, launch kits and trade show booths. It works by project, with fixed scope, timeline and price.

What 75 LAB does

  • POP display: floor, counter, clip strip and modular, with engineering and AR before production.
  • Islands and end caps (in Portuguese): the most contested spaces in the store, approved in the aisle in AR.
  • In-store kits (in Portuguese): piece by piece, with an assembly page and a QR code on the packaging.
  • Booths (in Portuguese): trade shows and events with an AR tour and immersive mode.
  • Augmented reality: the display at full scale in the store, on your phone, with no app.
  • POS material tracking: QR code, photo and location, so you know where each piece was installed.
  • Rich content, ClickLab (in Portuguese): the e-commerce product page treated as a brand piece.

How proven POS works

  1. Before production: each piece is approved in augmented reality, at full scale, on the retailer's own shelf, on a phone. Marketing, trade, sales and the retail buyer approve by seeing the piece, not a PDF.
  2. After installation: the piece can carry a QR code that opens the assembly page and records the store, date, photo and location, on a dashboard the brand follows live.

Cases

  • NGV: one display, four brands. The Universal Display adapted for Vitalitabs, De Llares, Bella Fio and Savedent, a four-sided island and the Conexão Farma booth, all in AR.
  • SEMP: 15 POS pieces, all in AR. The Split HW air conditioner kit on a single augmented reality page, with an assembly page and rich content for the marketplace.
  • All cases (in Portuguese): Savencia, Alltak and the full portfolio.
