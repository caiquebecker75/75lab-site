POP display

POP displays for brands, from design to store.

We create, engineer and produce displays that stand up, hold the product and arrive assembled. Before production, you see the display at full scale on your own shelf, on your phone.

75 LAB handles the entire point-of-purchase (POP) display for the point of sale (POS): concept, engineering, production with approved suppliers, and delivery. The difference is proof at both ends: the display is approved in augmented reality, at full scale, before you spend on production, and each unit can ship with a QR code that records where and when it was installed.

Types of displays we design

  • Floor display, freestanding, with shelves, peg hooks or trays.
  • Counter display for checkout, pharmacy and impulse spots.
  • Clip strip and peg bar for cross merchandising.
  • Modular display, reused from campaign to campaign by changing only the graphics.
  • Gravity-feed display, which restocks the product from the front.
  • Permanent fixture and showcase, in MDF, metal or acrylic.

How a project runs

  1. Immersion: product, weight, channel, store, budget and deadline. This is where the material gets decided.
  2. Concept and render with the brand's graphics applied.
  3. Engineering: technical drawings with views, dimensions, materials, thicknesses, load per shelf and tip-over stability.
  4. Augmented reality approval: a link opens the display at full scale on a phone, on the store floor or in the meeting room. Sales, trade and the buyer approve by seeing the unit, not a PDF.
  5. Production with suppliers approved by process (corrugated board, MDF, metal, acrylic, wire) and quality control.
  6. Delivery and assembly, with an assembly page and a QR code on the packaging for the installer, and an installation record with photo and location.

Why engineering comes before artwork

A beautiful display that tips over, a shelf that sags under the product and a unit that needs tools to assemble are the most expensive rework in trade marketing, because they only show up in the store. That is why every 75 LAB display goes through technical drawings, with checks on load, stability, tool-free assembly and shipping, before it goes into production.

To choose the material, see the guide Corrugated board, MDF, metal, acrylic or wire (in Portuguese). To check stability, see A display that does not tip over and a shelf that does not sag (in Portuguese).

Frequently asked questions

How long does a POP display project take?

It depends on the material, the quantity and the complexity of the unit. In the first conversation, 75 LAB sends back a schedule with the concept, engineering, AR approval, production and delivery stages, so you can plan the campaign backwards.

Do I need a 3D file to see the display in augmented reality?

No. If your brand already has the 3D model, we convert it for AR. If not, the model is built as part of our project. The link opens in the browser on iPhone and Android, with no app to install.

Does 75 LAB manufacture the display?

75 LAB designs the display and coordinates production with a network of suppliers approved by process, with quality control and a single person in charge from brief to delivery. You hire a project, not a list of suppliers.

Can the display be reused in another campaign?

Yes, when it is designed to be modular. The structure stays and only the graphics change. The Universal Display we adapted for four NGV brands is an example: the same module, with different artwork and hardware for each brand.

How do I know the display was set up in the store?

Each unit can ship with a QR code. The installer opens the assembly page and records the store, a photo and the location, and the brand follows everything on a dashboard with a map. See POS material tracking.
