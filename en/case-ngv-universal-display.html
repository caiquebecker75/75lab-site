Case · NGV

NGV: one display, four brands.

A single display module serves four brands, each with its own graphics and its own hardware. Put together, the modules become a four-sided island.

For NGV, 75 LAB adapted the Universal Display to the Vitalitabs, De Llares, Bella Fio and Savedent brands. The structure is the same; the artwork and the hardware change, with shelves for some and peg hooks for others. Four modules form a 112 × 112 × 170 cm island with one brand per side. Everything was published in augmented reality, together with the NGV booth for Conexão Farma.

The challenge

Four brands from different categories needed a presence at the point of sale (POS) without four projects, four molds and four suppliers. And each product calls for its own kind of display: bottles go on shelves, blister packs go on hooks.

Why a modular display

With a single module, the brand negotiates one structure, approves one engineering project and only swaps the graphics for each campaign or brand. The island is built from the same modules, with no new mold.
