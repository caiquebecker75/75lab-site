POS material tracking

Know where every point-of-sale piece was installed.

Point of sale (POS) material tends to leave the factory and vanish. With a QR code on the piece and a record at installation, the brand knows which store it went to, when, and how it was set up.

75 LAB tracks POS material with a QR code on every piece. The installer opens the assembly page and records the store with a photo and location, and the brand follows everything on a dashboard with a map, activated stores, installation pace and storefront photos. The client can get a live link, with no login.

Why it matters

Measuring the return on trade marketing is still the exception. In the Abratrade maturity survey released in November 2026, only 15.1% of professionals said they measure return in a standardized way (Giro News, in Portuguese). Without knowing whether the piece was installed, there is no way to measure the rest.

How it works

  1. Each piece ships with a QR code printed on the piece or on the packaging.
  2. The installer opens the assembly page and records name, store, a storefront photo and the phone's location.
  3. The record lands on a dashboard with a map, activated stores, daily pace, rankings by chain, city and state, and photos.
  4. The brand generates a live link for the client or for leadership, with no login, an expiry date and control over what is shown.

What the dashboard shows

  • Activated stores and coverage by chain, city and state.
  • Installation pace, day by day and cumulative.
  • Pieces installed per store, with a storefront photo.
  • Cross views of chain by project and of day by time.
  • Export to spreadsheet.

Frequently asked questions

How do you track POS material?

The simplest way is a QR code on each piece, linked to an assembly page. During installation, the field rep records the store, a photo and the location, and each record becomes a point on the brand's dashboard.

Does the field rep need to install an app?

No. The QR code opens a page in the phone's browser. The record asks for name, store and the device's location.

Can the client follow along without accessing the dashboard?

Yes. The brand generates a public link per campaign, live and with no login, with an expiry date and a choice of what appears, such as photos and the field reps' names.

Does it work for pieces 75 LAB did not produce?

Yes. The QR code and the assembly page can be applied to any piece in the campaign, as long as the label goes on the piece or the packaging before shipping.
