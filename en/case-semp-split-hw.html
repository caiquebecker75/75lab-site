SEMP: 15 point-of-sale pieces, all in AR.

The in-store kit for the Split HW air conditioner, from the archway to the floor mat, on a single augmented reality page, with an assembly page and rich content for the marketplace.

75 LAB brought the point of sale (POS) kit for the SEMP Split HW air conditioner into augmented reality: 15 pieces on a single page, which can be viewed one at a time, all together or at full scale. The same material became an assembly page with a QR code for the store and rich content for the product page on the marketplace.

The challenge

Major appliances sell through demonstration and through large spaces in the store. A 15-piece kit has to be understood as a set, by the people who approve it and the people who install it, and the same story has to carry on into e-commerce.

What 75 LAB did

  • 15 pieces in AR: archway, backdrop, premium island, cradle, cube, poster and band, shelf strip, spec sign, topper, side tag, header, lamá (curved standing panel), ceiling hanger, floor mat and checkout poster.
  • Piece showcase to present the kit: one piece at a time, all of them in a grid, or at full scale to compare.
  • Flat pieces in wall AR, such as the sign, topper and side tag applied to the unit.
  • Assembly page with a QR code for the entire kit.
  • Rich content for the product page on the marketplace.
